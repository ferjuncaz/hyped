Hyped — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Hyped ("the app") is a countdown app made by an independent developer. Your privacy matters, so the app is designed to collect as little as possible: nothing.

Data we collect

None. Hyped does not collect, store on any server, sell or share personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

The countdowns you create (name, emoji, date and theme) are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app and its widget. They are removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those transactions.

Sharing

When you tap "Share to Stories", the app creates an image on your device and opens the standard iOS share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if advertising is added in the future), this page will be updated and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions? Email ferjuncaz@yahoo.com.br.

Política de Privacidade (Português)
